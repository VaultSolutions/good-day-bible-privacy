Good Day Bible Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VaultSolutions handles information in the Good Day Bible mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Good Day Bible does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information from users. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Good Day Bible does not request access to your contacts, precise location, photos, camera, microphone, or other personal device content.

If the app saves settings, bookmarks, or preferences on your device, that information remains on the device and is not sent to VaultSolutions.

2. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Good Day Bible does not use advertising networks, analytics services, crash-reporting services, or tracking technologies that collect information about your use of the app.

3. Third-Party Services

Good Day Bible does not share personal information with third-party service providers. The app may be downloaded through an app store, such as Google Play. The app store may process information under its own privacy policy and terms.

4. Children’s Privacy

Good Day Bible is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal information, VaultSolutions does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Security and Retention

Because VaultSolutions does not receive or store personal information through Good Day Bible, there is no personal information from the app retained on VaultSolutions’ servers. App data stored locally on your device can generally be removed by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

6. Changes to This Policy

VaultSolutions may update this Privacy Policy if the app’s features or information practices change. The revised policy will be posted at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
